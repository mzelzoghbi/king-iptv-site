King IPTV Player

An IPTV player for Android that plays your own M3U playlist or Xtream Codes login. Live TV with a guide, films and series, and no ads, upsells or account to create.

Get it on Google Play Free · Android 7.0+ · iPhone and iPad coming

Important

King IPTV Player hosts and provides no content. It is an empty player. You supply your own playlist, an Xtream Codes login or an M3U link, from a service you already pay for. The app has no channels, no catalogue and nothing to watch until you add one.

What it does

Your playlist, your way

Xtream Codes credentials or an M3U / M3U8 link or file. Add several sources and switch between them in one tap.

Built for long lists

Thousands of channels and hundreds of categories stay searchable and scannable, with a category picker that never truncates a name.

Live TV with a guide

Live channels show what's on now and next from your provider's EPG. Favourites are kept per source.

Films and series

Films with plot and cast, series with seasons and episodes, and resume where you left off.

More channels play

ExoPlayer first, libVLC automatically when a stream defeats it. Picture-in-picture, subtitle and audio track choice.

Honest about the stream

Resolution, codecs, bitrate, buffer health and dropped frames, read from the player, not invented.

Quiet by design

Dark, precise and legible. No banner ads, no "Pro" badges, no trial counters, no monetisation surface at all.

Stays on your device

Your password is encrypted with an Android keystore key. Nothing is sent to us; there is no "us" to send it to.

Questions

Does King IPTV Player include any channels?

No. It is a player only. It hosts and provides no content, and shows nothing until you add a playlist from your own IPTV provider.

What playlist formats does it support?

Xtream Codes logins (server URL, username and password) and M3U or M3U8 playlists, either as a link or as a file. You can keep several sources and switch between them.

Does it have a TV guide (EPG)?

Yes. It reads your provider's EPG (XMLTV) and shows what is on now and next for each live channel.

Is it free?

Yes. It is free on Google Play, with no ads, no subscription and no in-app purchases.

Which devices does it run on?

Android phones and tablets running Android 7.0 or later. An iPhone and iPad version is coming.

Does it collect my data?

No. Your provider password is encrypted on the device and is sent only to the server you entered. There is no account, no analytics, no tracking and no server of ours. See the privacy policy.

Why won't a channel play?

The app tries ExoPlayer first and switches to libVLC automatically if a stream fails. If it still won't play, open Stream info in the player: it shows what the server actually sent, which usually tells you whether the problem is the app, your network or the provider.

Requirements

  • An Android phone or tablet running Android 7.0 or later
  • An active subscription with an IPTV provider, or your own M3U playlist

King IPTV Player is not affiliated with any provider and does not sell, resell or recommend one. Only add playlists you have the right to watch.

King IPTV Player

Your subscription, finally in a player that gets out of the way. No ads, no upsells, no account to create.

Important

King IPTV Player hosts and provides no content. It is an empty player. You supply your own playlist — an Xtream Codes login or an M3U link — from a service you already pay for. The app has no channels, no catalogue and nothing to watch until you add one.

What it does

Your playlist, your way

Xtream Codes credentials or an M3U link or file. Add several sources and switch between them in one tap.

Built for long lists

Thousands of channels and hundreds of categories stay searchable and scannable, with a category picker that never truncates a name.

Live, films and series

Live channels with now-playing information, films with plot and cast, series with seasons and episodes.

Honest about the stream

Resolution, codecs, bitrate, buffer health and dropped frames — read from the player, not invented.

Quiet by design

Dark, precise and legible. No banner ads, no "Pro" badges, no trial counters, no monetisation surface at all.

Stays on your device

Your credentials go in the iOS keychain. Nothing is sent to us — there is no "us" to send it to.

Requirements

  • iPhone or iPad running iOS 17 or later
  • An active subscription with an IPTV provider, or your own M3U playlist

King IPTV Player is not affiliated with any provider and does not sell, resell or recommend one.

Support

Questions, bugs or feedback: support@zaplatform.com.

If a channel will not play, open the player's Stream info panel — it reports what the server actually sent, which is usually enough to tell whether the problem is the app, the network or the provider.

Privacy Policy

King IPTV Player · last updated 1 September 2026

We collect nothing. King IPTV Player has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no servers of our own. Nothing you enter or watch is sent to ZaPlatform.

What stays on your device

All of the following is stored locally on your iPhone or iPad and is never transmitted to us:

Data Where it is kept
Provider password iOS keychain, encrypted by the system
Server address, username, playlist name App preferences on the device
Downloaded channel, film and series lists App storage, refreshed from your provider
Favourites, watch history, recent searches App preferences on the device

Deleting the app removes all of it. Removing a source from within the app deletes its password from the keychain.

What leaves your device

The app talks to exactly one kind of destination: the IPTV provider you chose. When you add a source and browse or play, your device contacts that provider directly to sign in, download the channel list, fetch channel artwork and stream video.

Those requests go to your provider's servers, not ours. Your provider can see them, and their own privacy policy governs what they do with that information. We have no visibility into it and no relationship with them.

The app also loads the Inter and JetBrains Mono typefaces bundled inside it, which requires no network access.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone. Content comes from your provider, and we cannot see, filter or rate it.

Third parties

There are no third-party SDKs in the app: no analytics, no advertising networks, no attribution or tracking frameworks. Nothing is shared with anyone, because nothing is collected in the first place.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to export, correct or delete. You control everything from the device: remove a source to delete its credentials, or delete the app to remove all of it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted here with a new date. The app will never begin collecting data without this page saying so first.

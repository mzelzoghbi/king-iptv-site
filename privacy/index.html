Privacy Policy

King IPTV Player · last updated 7 October 2026

We collect only anonymous usage statistics. From version 1.1, King IPTV Player uses Google's Firebase Analytics to count which screens are opened and whether streams play. There are no accounts, no advertising, no advertising ID and no servers of our own. What you enter and what you watch — logins, links, channel and title names — is never sent to ZaPlatform or to Google.

What stays on your device

All of the following is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to us:

Data Where it is kept
Provider password iOS / macOS keychain or Android keystore, encrypted by the system
Server address, username, playlist name App preferences on the device
Downloaded channel, film and series lists App storage, refreshed from your provider
Favourites, watch history, recent searches App preferences on the device

Deleting the app removes all of it. Removing a source from within the app deletes its password from the keychain or keystore.

What leaves your device

The app talks to two kinds of destination: the IPTV provider you chose, and Firebase Analytics. When you add a source and browse or play, your device contacts that provider directly to sign in, download the channel list, fetch channel artwork and stream video.

Those requests go to your provider's servers, not ours. Your provider can see them, and their own privacy policy governs what they do with that information. We have no visibility into it and no relationship with them.

Usage statistics (Firebase Analytics)

To find what breaks and what is used, the app sends Google's Firebase Analytics a small set of anonymous events:

Event What it contains
Screen viewed The screen's name, such as “Live TV” or “Settings”
Source added Its kind only: Xtream Codes, M3U link or M3U file
Playback started or failed Live, film or series; which decoder was used; the platform; for a failure, the decoder's error code

Firebase also records what it collects for every app: a random app instance ID (not your advertising ID, which the app never reads), the device model, operating system and app version, and a country or region estimated from your IP address. It is not linked to your name, email or provider account, and we do not use it for advertising. See how Firebase handles data.

The app never sends server addresses, usernames, passwords, playlist or stream links, or the names of channels, films, series or programmes.

The app also loads the Inter and JetBrains Mono typefaces bundled inside it, which requires no network access.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone — only the anonymous usage statistics described above. Content comes from your provider, and we cannot see, filter or rate it.

Third parties

Firebase Analytics, provided by Google as our service provider, is the only third-party SDK in the app. There are no advertising networks, no attribution or cross-app tracking frameworks, and nothing is sold or shared with anyone else.

Your rights

Everything you enter stays on the device: remove a source to delete its credentials, or delete the app to remove all of it. The usage statistics are anonymous and Google Analytics keeps them for at most 14 months; to have the statistics tied to your app instance deleted, email us at the address below.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted here with a new date. The app will never begin collecting data without this page saying so first.
